# GoreeCloud Launcher Benefits

## Current Development benefits

- **Authoritative workspace state:** rendered pages come from the Room workspace projection rather than a second UI-owned placement model.
- **Predictable failure behavior:** guarded movement and deletion fail closed when workspace preconditions are not satisfied.
- **Scalable page navigation:** the lazy page selector avoids an ever-growing eager Row and automatically keeps the selected page visible.
- **Useful context:** each page can show authoritative app counts and unsupported-item counts so users are not given a falsely empty picture of workspace state.
- **Native performance direction:** Android platform launchable-app APIs and Jetpack Compose are used directly for the first-party launcher surface.
- **Clear platform authority:** Android remains authoritative for installed/launchable activities while GoreeCloud Room state owns the accepted workspace arrangement.

## Intended longer-term benefits

GoreeCloud Launcher is intended to provide a polished, accessible, privacy-conscious home experience with rich organization, optional continuity, and deep GoreeCloud interoperability without turning background usage data into implicit tracking or creating competing workspace authorities.

Those longer-term benefits remain Development objectives until implementation and acceptance evidence exists.
